# Wavescope privacy policy

_Effective date: 25 October 2026_

Wavescope is made by **Rump dev** ("we"). This policy explains what
Wavescope does with information on your device. The short version: **your
measurements stay on your phone**. Wavescope has no account, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking.

## What Wavescope reads on your device

To show the signals around you, Wavescope reads data from your phone's sensors
and radios:

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise and approximate location** | Android only shares Wi-Fi, mobile-cell and satellite details with apps that have location access. Wavescope also uses location to draw signals on the live map while it is open and to compare the magnetic field with Earth's expected field where you are. | Stays on your device. |
| **Wi-Fi networks** (names, signal strength, channels) | To show nearby networks, channels and coverage. | Stays on your device. |
| **Mobile network cells** (bands, signal strength, cell identifiers, timing advance) | To show your mobile connection and estimate tower distance. | Stays on your device. |
| **Satellite status** (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou) | To show satellites overhead and signal strength. | Stays on your device. |
| **Bluetooth devices** (advertised names, vendor, signal strength) | To show devices and beacons nearby. | Stays on your device. |
| **NFC tags** (tag type, ID, stored data) | To show what a tag or card contains when you hold it to the phone. Data stored on bank cards and passports is never read. | Shown on screen only; not stored. |
| **Microphone audio** | Only on the Ultrasound screen and when you turn on the live map's Sound layer: to show inaudible sound, how loud it is and what kind of sound it is (for example traffic or birds), recognised by a model that runs on your phone. Audio is analysed live in memory. | **Never recorded or stored.** |
| **Magnetometer and motion sensors** | To measure magnetic fields. | Stays on your device. |

### The live map

While the Map tab is open, Wavescope combines your location with the mobile
network and magnetic measurements to draw coverage around you. Wi-Fi networks
and Bluetooth devices are never put on the map. With the Sound layer on, the map also
shows how loud it was and what kind of sound was recognised at each spot. This exists only in memory: it
is never saved, and it is gone when you press Start over or close the app.
Nothing is measured while the map is not on screen.

## Information that leaves your device

- **Map tiles.** To draw the map, Wavescope downloads map tiles from
  [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org). Like any website, the tile server
  receives your IP address and which map area is being viewed, which can
  reveal your approximate location. No measurements or identifiers
  are sent. OpenFreeMap's own privacy terms apply to those
  requests.
- **Optional donations** (Google Play version only). If you choose to
  donate, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your
  payment details.

Nothing else leaves your device. Wavescope contains no advertising,
analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

## Permissions

You can grant or refuse each permission, and change it any time in Android
settings. Screens that need a permission explain why before asking. Refusing a
permission only disables the parts that need it.

## Your choices and deletion

- The live map forgets everything when you press Start over or close the app.
- Clear all data in Android settings (Apps → Wavescope → Storage → Clear
  storage) or uninstall Wavescope; both remove your settings, the only thing it stores.
- Because we don't receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on
  our side.

## Children

Wavescope is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from them.
